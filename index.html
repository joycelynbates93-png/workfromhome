🚨 Protect Yourself From Work-From-Home Scams

  • Never pay someone to receive a job offer.
  • Never send gift cards or cryptocurrency to an alleged employer.
  • Verify that you are applying through the company's official career website.
  • Be cautious of recruiters who only communicate through personal email or messaging apps.
  • Never share sensitive personal information before verifying the employer.
  • Research the company and job posting before accepting an offer.
